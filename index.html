The method

Think the same way every time. Know the right theory. Trim your mistakes away.

Rating does not stall because you lack talent. It stalls because your thinking changes from move to move, your theory has holes, and the same mistakes come back game after game. The Hub fixes all three, in a fixed order.

1
Think

One fixed way to think: the MSPC system

The first T is the engine of everything: the same four questions, in the same order, on every move. Drilled on real positions until it runs by itself.

  1. MMove. What did my opponent’s last move change? New threats, new weaknesses.
  2. SSpecifics. Notice the structure and the features of the position, then make a plan.
  3. PPriorities. Checks, captures, attacks: the forcing moves first, for both sides.
  4. CCalculation. Verify the line until it is clear. Then play it.
2
Theory

The right theory for your level

Not “stop memorising openings”. The right theory at the right level: from about 800 to 1200 the Colle-Koltanowski, Caro-Kann and Semi-Slav, plus middlegame structures and plans and endgame technique. Every line is read with MSPC.

3
Trim

Cut your own mistakes away

Full analysis of your own games finds the blunders, bad habits and leaks, sorts them by MSPC step and turns them into Working Points you train until they stop coming back.

Put those three together and the things that keep players stuck, random thinking, patchy theory and repeated mistakes, disappear one by one. This is exactly how a 355 became a 2105.
