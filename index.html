    Questions

    Do I need to download anything from a store?

    No. Lanckriet Chess is a web app. Open it in your browser, then add it to your home screen or desktop in one tap. It then opens full screen with its own icon.

    Which devices does it work on?

    Android, iPhone and iPad, Windows and Mac. Any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox) can run it. Installing is available in Chrome, Edge, Safari and most Android browsers.

    What is the difference between Pro and VIP?

    Nothing in the app: VIP has exactly the same benefits and the same Founding and Early Adopter price tranches as Pro. It is for members who want to contribute extra to the project.

    How do I cancel?

    Use the subscription link in the pricing section, or message @lanckrietchess on Instagram. A subscription stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

    How does my membership switch on after I pay?

    Sign in to the app with the same email address you used here. Pro or VIP, and any tokens, switch on by themselves.

    What happens to my data?

    You can delete your account and the data linked to it any time: in the app under Account, or on the account deletion page.
