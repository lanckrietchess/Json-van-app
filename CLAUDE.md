# Lanckrietchess Training Hub

Chess training web app by Kyenzo (lanckrietchess, certified chess coach), live at https://lanckrietchesstrainerhub.vercel.app (landing page `/`, the app `/app.html`, cache wiper `/reset.html`), also installable as a PWA (web-first; no store billing). A `*-<hash>-lanckrietchess.vercel.app` address is a frozen per-deployment link: never install or bookmark one. HANDOFF.md is the V5-era handoff (history only). The current reference is `PROJECT_KNOWLEDGE.md` in the Claude project: read it first. GitHub (`lanckrietchess/Json-van-app`) is the single source of truth for code: always fetch the live files from GitHub before a code change, never build on an attached or remembered copy.

## Architecture rules (never break these)

- The whole app is one self-contained `app.html` (`index.html` is the landing page): vanilla JavaScript, Tailwind from its CDN, chess.js and chessboard.js from cdnjs. No build step, no framework, no bundler, no npm dependencies for the site.
- Dark slate design (#0f172a background, #1e293b cards, #38bdf8 accents), top-bar navigation, mobile first (check 390px wide).
- No hardcoded course content. Everything players study comes from `content.json` in the GitHub repo `lanckrietchess/Json-van-app`, the single source of truth. Never add, change or remove course content (repertoires, chapters, lines, positions, lessons) in code or in `content.json`: the courses must stay 100% faithful to Kyenzo's ChessTempo exports. Exception approved by Kyenzo on 17 Nov 2026: the coaching texts of the Lichess-based endgame and middlegame drills (`lc-*`, `mg-lc-*`) are generated from the board and Stockfish by `tools/course_truth.mjs` (positions, solutions and chapters stay as they are); the opening trees are never touched. Second exception, asked by Kyenzo on 25 Nov 2026: `tools/repertoire_build.py` adds the lines of his newer ChessTempo export (`sources/ct-all-repertoires-2026-11-25.pgn`, moves and comments exactly as exported) and short lines for the positions he met at least 5 times in his own games that no chapter covered (his own move when Stockfish keeps it within 35 centipawns, otherwise Stockfish's, notes that say which); existing nodes and their notes are never changed or removed. Third exception, asked by Kyenzo on 26 and 27 Nov 2026: the old Lichess-puzzle middlegame and endgame drills (`lc-*`, `mg-lc-*`) are replaced by examples found per rule / lesson by a board test, from his own games (`kg-*`, `mg-kg-*`) and the Lichess database (`ldb-*`, `mg-ldb-*`), `tools/course_examples.py`, each one checked by Stockfish, and the lessons' exercise `steps` point at them, and the Endgame / Middlegame Labs (`data/eg-lab.json`, `data/mg-lab.json`) add positions to know by heart, checklists per endgame type and per sacrifice, and plans per pawn structure; the lessons themselves (`courses.endgame`, `courses.middlegame`) are unchanged.
- Admin settings live in the `v5` section of `content.json`, written by the hub's own admin mode (Publish). Don't hardcode them.
- Stockfish runs in the player's browser (`engine/`, a Web Worker). No server-side engine.
- No AI answer is on the owner's bill (9.82.0, bring your own key): every AI answer in the app (free coach, Pro coach, funnel bots, the owner's content / teacher bots, Make My Own, Game-to-Script's "Write the script with my key") runs on the player's OWN key through `LC92` (or Chrome's built-in AI), straight from the browser to the provider, never through our server. Without a key `AI.on()` is false and every caller keeps its hardcoded path (LC83, the course links, the button menus). The Arena's `chat` action answers `byok` unless the env `SERVER_AI=on`. The only server AI left is the community chat moderation (`MOD_AI_DAILY_CAP`, 0 = off). Never put the owner's key into the page for members: anyone could read it.
- No secrets in the repo. ADMIN_TOKEN, ANTHROPIC_API_KEY and PID_SALT live in Supabase secrets (`supabase secrets set`); never change PID_SALT once players exist. `server/admin-token.txt` is git-ignored.
- UI copy is English, short and plain, in Kyenzo's coaching voice. Keep the existing tone. The founder text shows a 2105 peak rating.
- Owner tools are permanent: the floating mode switcher (`LC49`, bottom right) and its "Test as Owner / Pro / Free" switch (`LC53`) are never removed or covered in a UI cleanup. The owner account is `lanckrietchess@gmail.com`; the release suite checks both.
- Value first: no email or sign-in ask before a person's first finished game review. `LC53` asks once, after the review and the lesson, then guides to the MSPC Framework, the coach's `?` and the Game Library.

- The honest coach (9.56.0): `LC83.diagnose(r)` (`lc83.js`) explains ONE mistake from the board plus Stockfish, with no AI (`r` = fen, color, played, bestUci, replyUci, and when known bestPv, replyPv, replyMate, prevFen, oppUci, threatUci, memKey). It names the cause in a fixed order (allowedMate, missedMate, blocker, ignoredThreat, leftDefence, hanging, doubleAttack, missedTactic, positional), then the Coach Brain (`brain`) reads Stockfish's two lines move by move (`walk`): motifs (fork, pin, skewer, discovered attack, removing the defender, deflection, their in-between check, trapped piece, promotion, back-rank / smothered mate, a quiet killer), what the line wins or loses, a forced mate in the line, and replaces a weak cause with the motif it finds; quiet positions get true structure / opening / endgame facts (`quietFacts`). It also names the human blind spot (`blind`: hope chess, greed, a fake pin, the expected recapture, a backward / long / quiet move, the point came later, the other wing; or, when nothing punished the move, why the better move was missed: a knight jump, a backward move, a sacrifice, an attack that is not a capture, a quiet move), a puzzle theme to train (`train`, opened in the theme trainer through `sessionStorage` `lc-tp-open` + `#themes`) and the player's recurring pattern (`remember` / `patternOf`, localStorage `lc83-brain`, Trim reviews of the player's own games only).
