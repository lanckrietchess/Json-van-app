Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

LanckrietChess Training Hub ("we", "our", or "us") operates the LanckrietChess Training Hub mobile application and web platform. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. Information Collection and Use

We collect minimal information necessary to provide and improve our service to you. When you register or use our application, we may ask you to provide certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you, such as your email address for account authentication and subscription management.

2. Use of Data

LanckrietChess Training Hub uses the collected data for various purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our service
  • To notify you about changes to our service
  • To allow you to participate in interactive features of our application
  • To provide customer support and manage subscriptions or training access
  • To monitor the usage of the service and detect, prevent and address technical issues

3. Data Storage and Cloud Synchronization

To ensure your chess progress, ratings, and working points are saved across devices, your training data and profile state are securely backed up and synchronized using Supabase cloud storage. This cloud backup feature is enabled by default to protect your data against device loss or browser resets, but it can be managed or toggled via your in-app settings hub.

4. Email updates and the bonus key

Marketing emails (coaching tips, new lessons and offers) are optional. We only send them if you tick the box yourself. The box is never ticked in advance, and you can say no and keep using the app. If you tick it, we store your email address, the date and where you agreed, and we send you a welcome email with a bonus key worth 1 token through an email delivery service. You can unsubscribe at any time in the app under Account > Email updates, and we then stop sending marketing emails.

The bonus key can be used once per account and once per email address, so we keep a record of the redemption (account id, email address and date). The legal basis for the marketing emails is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time without affecting the service. The controller is Kyenzo Lanckriet, Middelkerke, Belgium.

5. Chess ratings, milestones, results and messages

If you link your Chess.com or Lichess user name, our server reads your public Rapid rating from that site now and then. We keep the user name, your starting rating, your highest rating since you joined and the time of the last check. We use this to give you token rewards at every hundred rating points (and a one-time catch-up bonus if you join above 900), and to prevent the same chess account from being used by several members.

A result and a message are only shown on our website if you tick the box for it when you claim a milestone. We then show your first name, the chess site, your starting and highest rating, the number of days and, if you wrote one and the owner approved it, your message. We never show your email address. The owner checks every result and message before it appears. You can take your result off the website at any time in the app under Account.

6. Library purchases, printed books and the Guest Library

When you buy a book, book summary or video course in the Books Vault, we store your email address, the item, the date and how you paid (tokens, Stripe or a gift from the owner), so the item stays in your library on every device. Card payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your card.

If you order a paperback or hardcover, Stripe collects your name and shipping address and passes them to us so we can send the book. If you are a VIP member and claim your free hardcover, you type your name and address in the app for the same reason. We use the address only to send that book, and we wipe it 90 days after the book was shipped (or the order was cancelled).

Creators who offer an item in the Guest Library give us their name, a short bio, their email address and the content. We use their email address only to talk to them about the item; members see the creator name and bio, never the email address. Guest items are checked by the owner before they are shown.

7. The 7-day trial, payments and saved cards

The Pro trial costs €0 for 7 days and needs a card. Stripe (our payment provider) keeps the card; we never see or store card numbers. We keep only your Stripe customer number, your e-mail address and the dates of the trial, so Pro switches on and off correctly and Stripe can offer your saved card at your next purchase. Payments, refunds and cancellations run through Stripe. Legal basis: the contract with you.

8. Where you came from (links and campaigns)

When you arrive through one of our links (for example /l/igb or a link with utm_source), we keep that source and a random device number on your device and count one visit per day. When you sign in, we link that source to your account so we can see which videos and posts bring people who stay. We never use it for advertising networks or sell it. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in measuring our own channels. Ask us and we delete it.

9. Coach chat, game reviews, achievements and community safety

Messages between you and Kyenzo in the coach chat are stored so both of you can read them, and only Kyenzo can see your messages. If you send a game for a Priority Game Review, we store the game, your note and your choices: a public video review and your first name are used only if you ticked those boxes (16 or older, or with a parent’s agreement). “Share my results” is off until you switch it on; when you answer “Yes, share it” to a Congratulations message, your first name and that achievement appear in the community feed and may be picked for the Wall of Love on our website. You can switch it off any time in Account. Private messages from other members are shown only after you accept the chat. When you block someone we store the block; a member blocked by three different people within a week gets an automatic chat ban (1 week, then 1 month, 1 year, permanent), which the owner can review. Rivals, tribes and weekly XP show your first name and XP to your rivals and tribe members.

10. Security of Data

The security of your data is important to us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our official channels or support platform associated with LanckrietChess.
