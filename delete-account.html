Delete your account

This page is for the Lanckriet Chess app (Training Hub) by Kyenzo Lanckriet. You can delete your account and the data linked to it here, without installing the app.

Fastest: inside the app

Open the app, go to Account, and tap Delete my account. Type DELETE to confirm. Your account, progress, tokens and email login are removed straight away.

Or ask here

Enter the email address you sign in with. We delete the login and your email-updates choice, and the data linked to it, within 30 days. If you also used the app without signing in, delete that data inside the app (Account, Delete my account): it is stored under the device, not under an email address.

What is deleted, what is kept

  • Deleted: your profile and progress, level and XP, rating snapshots, friends and referrals, badges, tokens and game history on our servers, your email login and your email-updates choice.
  • Kept: records of payments and licences, because tax and refund rules require it. They no longer point at you. Backups are overwritten on their normal cycle.

Have a subscription?

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it first: manage or cancel my subscription. If you need help, message @lanckrietchess on Instagram and we will do it for you.
