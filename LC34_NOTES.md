# LC34 / hub 9.12.0: launch layer

Page: `index.html`, `sw.js`, `manifest.webmanifest` (built from 9.11.0 with `apply_patches_v34.py`, 15 exact-match hooks).
Server: `server-lc34/` (`arena.js`, `stripe.js`, the SQL migration, the two patch scripts). `arena.js` also contains the Carousel voice action from 9.10.0.

## Deploy order (do not skip a step)
1. **SQL:** run `server-lc34/20261026120000_lc34_funnel.sql` (one new table, `arena.lc34_pro_sales`). Idempotent.
2. **Arena function:** back up, then deploy `server-lc34/arena.js`. New secrets are optional: `TOKENS_FREE_BOOST` (3), `TOKENS_FREE_BOOST_DAYS` (7; `0` switches the habit week off), `TOKENS_FREE` (1).
3. **Stripe function:** back up, then deploy `server-lc34/stripe.js` (same `stripe_index.ts`, still `--no-verify-jwt`). Secrets already there: `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`, `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, `ADMIN_TOKEN`. Make sure `ALLOWED_ORIGINS` contains `https://lanckrietchess-app.vercel.app` and `APP_URL` is the app URL with a trailing slash. Optional: `PRO_YEARLY_BASE_CENTS` (40000), `PRO_MONTHLY_CENTS` (3900), `TRANCHE1_CAP` / `TRANCHE2_CAP` (100 / 100), `TRANCHE1_CENTS` / `TRANCHE2_CENTS` (20000 / 30000), `HOLD_MINUTES` (35), `LICENSE_GRACE_DAYS` (2), `PRO_DAILY_TOKENS` (10), `STRIPE_TAX=inclusive` (turns on automatic tax with tax-inclusive prices; needs Stripe Tax set up).
4. **Stripe Dashboard, webhook** `https://<project>.supabase.co/functions/v1/stripe/webhook`: events `checkout.session.completed`, `checkout.session.expired`, `invoice.paid`, `charge.refunded` (the first and last were already used by creator support).
5. **Admin mode:** set **Google Play page of the app** (`links.playStore`). Empty = the Android overlay stays off (no dead button).
6. Deploy the page files.
7. **Run one purchase in Stripe test mode before launch** (card 4242 4242 4242 4242): checkout, return to the app, key switches Pro on, Founding badge shows, then refund it and confirm the key stops working.

## NOT tested, and what you must know before launch
1. **Google Play Billing is not built.** A subscription bought inside the Play app must use Google Play Billing; the app must not send users to Stripe. I therefore show no checkout in the Play app: members can enter a key, nothing else. Play users cannot subscribe in the app until Billing is built (client, server verification against the Google Play Developer API, and Play Console products per tranche). Also check the existing "you upgrade inside the Skool community" wording on the Upgrades page: that outside-payment steering inside the Play app can be a review problem.
2. **Real Stripe and the real database function.** Tested against a mock Stripe and against my own copies of `lc_redeem_key` / `lc_license_status` / `_lc_create_license` with the documented argument list and answer shape. Step 7 above covers this.
3. **The key is not emailed.** It is delivered on the success screen and recovered on the same browser if the tab was closed. A buyer who pays on another device, or clears their browser first, has no way to get the key yet. A Make/Supabase email on `checkout.session.completed` is the fix.
4. **Tax and legal.** Prices are plain amounts unless you turn on `STRIPE_TAX`. "Lifetime Founding Rate" is implemented as a rate locked while the subscription stays active, not as lifetime access. EU consumer withdrawal-right wording for digital subscriptions is not in the checkout. Have these reviewed.
5. **"First 100" counts places held in open checkouts** for up to 35 minutes, so the counter can briefly show fewer places than will finally sell.
6. **Habit week is per device.** A new device id gets a new week of 3 tokens. Cost is low (no external AI per free action), but it can be farmed.
7. A lapsed Founding member who re-subscribes later pays the then-current price (their badge is kept, the price lock is not).
8. Other badge display spots (friend cards, leaderboards) are not wired: other players cannot see your badge yet.
9. The Upgrades-page sales assistant and the Tier 3 mentorship application remain reachable from the Upgrades page; only the pop-up triggers were unified.
